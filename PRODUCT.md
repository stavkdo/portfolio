# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output), chosen by the user. Editable content lives in `src/data/content.ts`; each section is a component in `src/components/`, client behaviour in `src/scripts/`.

## Users

- **Recruiters and employers** evaluating Stav for development or cyber security roles. They usually arrive from a CV or a profile link and decide quickly whether to look further.
- **University and program admissions** (degrees, special programs, tech units) looking for evidence of ability, curiosity and character.
- **Friends and community**: people who know Stav and want to see who they are and what they make.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Stav. It has to make them stand out ("לבלוט"), and show a whole person: a cellist, a developer and someone who studies cyber security, all through a love of fantasy. Success means a visitor remembers Stav afterwards and can say what they do.

## Positioning

The combination is the point: music (cello), software development and cyber security, presented together through a fantasy sensibility. Most developer portfolios show only the code. This one shows how the three disciplines inform each other.

## Operating Context

Visitors come from links in CVs, applications and social profiles, on desktop and on phones. Recruiters and admissions readers skim; friends explore.

## Product Principles

1. Show the whole person: music, code and security belong together.
2. Prove instead of claim: real projects and interactive demonstrations over adjectives.
3. Clear at a skim, rewarding to explore. A recruiter should grasp who Stav is in seconds; a curious visitor should find more the longer they stay.
4. Honest content only: placeholders stay clearly marked until real material replaces them.
